What do we know about Rwanda return hubs and how effective will they be?

Five EU countries have announced a controversial plan to set up a center in Rwanda for migrants with no legal right to stay in Europe.

Western World News · November 20, 2026

Five EU countries have announced a controversial plan to set up a center in Rwanda for migrants with no legal right to stay in Europe. While supporters say it will help governments to deter illegal immigration, critics are concerned about potential human rights violations and a lack of oversight.
