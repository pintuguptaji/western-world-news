Spain frees wealthy US-sought activist on bail as Irish police raid Dublin home

Pro-Palestinian donor James ‘Fergie’ Chambers released as court considers extradition request.

By Western World News Editorial Team · November 20, 2026

Pro-Palestinian donor James ‘Fergie’ Chambers released as court considers extradition request. In a blur of events surrounding the Trump administration’s effort to extradite James “Fergie” Chambers, Spain released the wealthy US activist and donor on bail even as Irish police raided a Dublin house owned by Chambers and his partner, Stella Schnabel, acting on a request from US authorities.
