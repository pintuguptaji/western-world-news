Live: Ukraine war

Rolling updates on Russia's war in Ukraine: the overnight missile strikes, the Shenzhen summit and Europe's response. Times are in CET. Last updated November 20, 2026.

Nov 20, evening

Youth-led protests sweep Europe

Tens of thousands of young people marched in more than 25 countries on Friday, calling for more spending on schools and public services and less on the military, with the largest marches in Italy and Germany. Organizers say the demonstrations will continue through the weekend.

Nov 20, afternoon

Moscow rules out NATO-style guarantees for Ukraine

Russia’s deputy foreign minister, Sergei Ryabkov, said any “quasi-membership of NATO” for Ukraine was unacceptable, pushing back on claims from the US negotiating team that Moscow was comfortable with Article 5-style security guarantees.

Nov 20, morning

Coalition allies pledge €70bn for Ukraine in 2027

The 40 countries in the “coalition of the willing” meeting in Berlin committed to honouring NATO’s pledge of €70 billion in military aid to Ukraine this year, and to keeping it at at least that level in 2027, despite pressure from Washington.

Nov 20, 10:33

Embassies damaged in Kyiv barrage

A daytime Russian barrage blew out windows at the Austrian and Dutch missions in Kyiv and damaged a cathedral, prompting Brussels to summon Moscow's envoy.

Nov 20, 09:15

Netherlands summons Russian ambassador

The Dutch government summoned Russia's ambassador after its embassy in Kyiv suffered minor damage when a projectile landed near the building. No one was hurt.

Nov 20, 08:00

Poland scrambles jets as airspace is violated

Poland said fighter jets were scrambled and Patriot air defense systems activated early on Friday after objects flew into and fell inside Polish territory during the overnight attack.

Nov 20, 06:30

More than 110 missiles fired at Ukraine overnight

Ukraine said Russia fired at least 110 missiles and around 400 drones overnight, an unusually large number even amid frequent aerial assaults. At least nine people were killed and 28 injured, and three of Kyiv's bridges were damaged.

Nov 19, 22:10

Trump tells European leaders Putin is “tired” and ready for a deal

After the Shenzhen talks, President Trump spoke with the “coalition of the willing” in Berlin. According to a senior European official, his main message was that “Putin is tired, wants to end this, ready for a deal.”

Nov 19, 18:40

E3 leaders call for winter energy ceasefire

The leaders of the UK, Germany and France and Ukraine called on Russia to agree to a ceasefire on attacks on energy infrastructure for the duration of winter.

Nov 19, 17:00

Trilateral talks end without breakthrough

Trump, Xi and Putin met in Shenzhen for just over an hour. A joint declaration covered UN reform, nuclear weapons and oil production, but produced no breakthrough on Ukraine and no official declaration was released.
